Two Population Dust Evolution

Intro

The dust evolution is based on the two population dust method fromTil Birnstiel's two-pop-py. The full dust grain size distribution is represented as just fine and coarse grains. The fine grains have a constant and uniform size, while the coarse grains are able to grow and evolve according to local conditions.

Physical Size Limits

The coarse grains have four potential conditions for their maximum size:

  • The growth limit - \(a_{\rm{growth}}\)
  • The drift limit - \(a_{\rm{drift}}\)
  • The fragmentation limit - \(a_{\rm{frag}}\)
  • The drift-induced fragmentation limit - \(a_{\rm{df}}\)

The size of the large grains are first limited by their growth rate. The grains grow exponentially and their size can be expressed as

$$a_{\rm{growth}} = a_0 \cdot \exp{\frac{t}{\tau_{\rm{growth}}}}, \; \tau_{\rm{growth}}=\frac{a}{\dot{a}} \simeq \frac{1}{\Omega_K (\Sigma_d/\Sigma_g)} $$

If the radial drift of coarse grains occurs on a similar or shorter timescale than the grains can grow and replenish, then the drift size limit can be solved as

$$a_{\rm{ drift}} = f_d \frac{2 \Sigma_{d} V_{K}^2}{\pi \rho_s c_{s}^2} \gamma^{-1}.$$

The maximum grain size may also be limited by the fragmentation of dust grains due turbulent motion. In this case, the representative size may be stated as

$$a_{\rm{ frag}} = f_f \frac{2 \Sigma_{g} u_{f}^2}{3 \pi \rho_s \alpha_t c_{s}^2}.$$

Finally, the relative drift rate of similar sized grains may exceed the fragmentation velocity of the grains, which sets the drift-induced fragmentation limit to be

$$a_{\rm{ df}} = \frac{2 u_f V_K}{\gamma c_s^2}.$$

Setup

The .opt file

The Multifluid package for FARGO3D allows users to add any number of dust species as pressureless fluids. To utilize the two-population method, users must specify three total fluids in the .opt file: the gas and two dust fluids. The .opt file should therefore have the following block: 

    FLUIDS := 0 1 2
    NFLUIDS = 3
    FARGO_OPT += -DNFLUIDS=${NFLUIDS}

FARGO3D can treat the disk viscosity using either a constant kinematic viscosity, \( \nu \), or a constant alpha viscosity, \( \alpha_t\), as defined by the Shakura-Sunyaev (1973) viscosity prescription. Our dust size limits depend on \( \alpha_t\). Therefore, the following line should be included: 

    FARGO_OPT += -DALPHAVISCOSITY

The following additions to the .opt file will include the calculation for the maximum grain size of the large population and the transfer of mass between the populations, respectively. 

    FARGO_OPT += -DSTOKES2POP
    FARGO_OPT += -DDUSTMASSTRANSFER

The .par file

Additional parameters for the dust evolution can be specified in the .par file. All dust parameters are expressed using cgs units. The dust parameters are as follows:

  • RHO_S: The material volume density of the dust
  • Dust_a1: Grain size of the small grain population (fixed)
  • Dust_a2: Initial grain size of the large grain population
  • Vfrag: fragmentation velocity of grains

Implementation

Particle sizes are updated in the Collisions() function in collisions.c prior to the update to the dusts' velocities. The calculation of the grain sizes (and the corresponding Stokes numbers) for each 2D grid cell occurs in Dust_grain_sizes.h. Mass transfer between the two dust species due to fragmentation of large grains and sweep-up of small grains is calculated in Dust_mass_transfer.h
